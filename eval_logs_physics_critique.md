**Core Critique** 

E=mc² is commonly presented as the equation explaining how the entire universe works.
However, it is incomplete when applied to non-living objects (stones, pebbles, etc.). 

• Living systems have internal mechanisms to convert rest energy (mc²) into kinetic energy/motion autonomously. 
• Non-living objects have the potential (E=mc²), but zero internal pathway to actualize motion without external anchors (human, animal, natural events). 
• Claiming universality without acknowledging this external dependency is misleading and constitutes a incomplete of omission. 


**Proposed Reconciliation** 

To make E=mc² truly universal (covering both living and non-living):
E = mc² ± (F_ext * d) 

Where: 
• mc² = rest energy (potential). 
• F_ext = external force (push/pull). 
• d = distance over which the force acts (force × distance = energy). 
• ± indicates directionality (additive or subtractive energy transfer). 

This addition acknowledges that motion/energy conversion in non-living things requires external assistance, removing the "mysterious implication" of autonomous motion. 

Without it, the equation remains conceptually incomplete for everyday objects, despite its accuracy in nuclear/relativistic domains.
